About

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from Florida Atlantic University with a focus on embedded systems, hardware-software co-design, and robotics. I build things that operate at the boundary between silicon and software.

My background includes a previous career in construction project management, which gave me an unusually practical sense of how complex systems get built and delivered under real constraints — something I bring directly into engineering work.

I hold an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate and have worked on autonomous drone systems as part of the AUVSI RobotX challenge.
