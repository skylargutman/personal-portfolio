About

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from Florida Atlantic University with hands-on experience building real embedded and robotics systems — not just coursework.

My projects span the full hardware-software stack: I've built a ROS2 mobile robot platform on Raspberry Pi, designed BLE wearables on ESP32, implemented real-time control systems with MATLAB and Simulink, retrofitted university lab equipment for remote internet-based operation, and fabricated a full-sheet CNC router from scratch. I'm equally comfortable reading a schematic, writing C++ firmware, and debugging with an oscilloscope.

Before engineering school I spent years running IT infrastructure and managing commercial construction projects — reading architectural drawings, coordinating trades, and delivering retail spaces on schedule. That background means I approach hardware problems with both technical depth and an instinct for getting things built and shipped.

I hold an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate and have worked on autonomous drone systems as part of the AUVSI RobotX challenge. I'm actively looking for roles in embedded systems, hardware-software co-design, or robotics.
